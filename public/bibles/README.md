# Included Bible texts

The King James Version (KJV) and American Standard Version of 1901 (ASV) are loaded on demand, never in the initial JavaScript bundle.

Data source: https://github.com/thiagobodruk/bible

Rights in the KJV are administered under Crown prerogative in the United Kingdom; check local requirements before redistribution or public use there. No proprietary contemporary translations are included. Verify verses against your church's preferred edition before public presentation.
